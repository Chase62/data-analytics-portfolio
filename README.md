# Featured Projects
## Identifying the Best Properties for Investment in Michigan → [View Repository](https://github.com/Chase62/michigan-real-estate)
* Busness Question: What attributes of properties results in the highest potential return on investment?
* Tools: Google Sheets, SQL, Tableau
* Key Findings: Single family homes that are new builds (<10 years) and mid-life properties (25–50 years) sized 1,000–3,000 sqft perform best. Properties located in the Marquette, Grand Rapids, and Pontiac areas should be prioritized.


## Cyclistic: How Different Users Utilize a Bike Share? → [View Repository](https://github.com/Chase62/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)
* Busness Question: How do casual riders vs. annual members use the service differently?
* Tools: Google Sheets, BigQuery, SQL, Tableau
* Key Findings: Casual riders take longer rides, primarily in the afternoons and weekends, typically frequenting tourist and recreational hotspots. While Annual Members predominently utilize the service for commuting, with peak activity during the morning and evening rush hour.
